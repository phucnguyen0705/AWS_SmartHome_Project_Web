# Summary Report: "Event 1: The 8-Team AWS Challenge"

### Event Purpose

- Host a competitive arena for 8 teams to test practical AWS knowledge.
- Deepen and validate core understanding of foundational AWS services (DynamoDB, S3, EC2...).
- Train quick decision-making, problem-solving, and teamwork under strict time limits.


### Competition Format & Rules

#### 8-Team Elimination Battle
- **8 teams** compete directly through rounds of challenging technical questions to accumulate points.
- **Elimination Mechanism**: Lower-scoring teams are gradually eliminated after each round, creating an intense and high-stakes atmosphere.

#### Core Knowledge Topics
- **Amazon EC2**: Selecting appropriate instance types, cost optimization strategies (Spot/On-Demand/Reserved), auto-scaling policies, and operational troubleshooting.
- **Amazon S3**: Storage class lifecycle management, bucket security/IAM policies, multipart uploads, and performance tuning.
- **Amazon DynamoDB**: Data modeling (Single-table design), Partition/Sort key strategies, GSI/LSI implementation, RCU/WCU provisioning, and read consistency models.


### Key Learnings & Takeaways

#### Deepening Technical Knowledge
- **Identifying Common Pitfalls**: Uncovered subtle edge cases in EC2, S3, and DynamoDB configurations through tricky competition questions.
- **Optimization Mindset**: Understanding not just *how* to use AWS services, but how to architect them efficiently according to the AWS Well-Architected Framework.

#### Teamwork & Decision-Making Under Pressure
- **Time Management**: Making swift, consensus-driven decisions within strict time bounds per question.
- **Strategic Execution**: Learning how to manage risk, capitalize on high-value questions, and maintain composure through elimination rounds.


### Event Experience

- **High-Energy Atmosphere**: The fierce battle among 8 teams created a dynamic and engaging environment with constant scoreboard shifts.
- **Peer Learning**: Observing how other teams analyzed questions provided valuable perspectives on technical problem-solving.
- **Practical Relevance**: Questions were grounded in real-world engineering scenarios rather than dry theoretical concepts.

### Key Takeaways & Personal Experience
#### Key Takeaways
- **Identifying Knowledge Blind Spots**: Observing teams tackle complex scenario-based questions revealed practical edge cases in DynamoDB (Single-table design), S3 (Lifecycle & Bucket Policies), and EC2 (Cost Optimization) that I previously overlooked.
- **Problem-Solving Under Pressure**: Watching top-performing teams break down problems, eliminate wrong choices, and lock in answers quickly provided valuable lessons on fast, accurate AWS decision-making.
- **Importance of Team Synergy**: Noticed that teams with clear domain distribution (e.g., dedicated experts in Database vs. Compute/Networking) exhibited higher confidence and lower error rates.

#### Personal Experience & Observations
- **Active Observation & Self-Assessment**: Throughout the 8-team competition, I actively formulated my own answers prior to the teams' submissions to benchmark my current AWS knowledge.
- **Learning from Real-World Scenarios**: Noted down tricky technical questions and troubleshooting scenarios presented during the event as reference material for my ongoing technical development.
