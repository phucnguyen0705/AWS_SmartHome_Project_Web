### Overall Evaluation

1. **Working Environment**
   The working environment is friendly and open. FCAJ members are always willing to support me even outside working hours. The workspace is tidy and comfortable, which helps improve my concentration. However, it would be even better if the company could add a few social gatherings or team-bonding activities to strengthen connection among members.

2. **Support from Mentor / Team Admin**
   The mentor provides dedicated guidance, explains concepts clearly, and always encourages me to ask questions. The admin team supports administrative procedures and documents thoughtfully. I really appreciate that the mentor creates opportunities for me to face challenges and solve problems on my own before offering suggestions.

3. **Relevance of Work to Academic Major**
   The assigned tasks align closely with what I learned at university, while also expanding my knowledge with practical real-world skills. Thanks to this, I was able to both reinforce my foundation and enhance my professional capabilities.

4. **Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**
   The internship helped me cultivate many practical skills: using project management tools, teamwork, and professional communication. The career guidance and insights shared by my mentor also boosted my confidence regarding my career path.

5. **Company Culture & Team Spirit**
   The company culture is very positive: everyone respects one another, working seriously while maintaining a pleasant atmosphere. During peak project periods, everyone collaborates and supports each other regardless of their position, making me feel genuinely connected to the team.

6. **Internship Policies / Benefits**
   The company accommodates flexible internship working hours. Additionally, having the opportunity to join internal training sessions is a major plus.

### Additional Questions

* **What did you find most satisfying during your internship?**  
  What satisfied me the most was the dedication of my Mentor and the FCAJ team. I was given the autonomy to manage my work and the opportunity to try out real-world solutions.

* **What do you think the company should improve for future interns?**  
  The company could consider organizing more short events or team-building activities to foster engagement.

* **If recommending to a friend, would you suggest they intern here? Why or why not?**  
  I would definitely recommend interning here because the working environment is very open, the support system for interns is great, working hours are flexible, and most importantly, there are valuable opportunities to learn directly from experienced mentors.
